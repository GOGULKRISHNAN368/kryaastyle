# Kryaa Styles - Luxury Indian Designer Fashion

Featuring the pleasant warm champagne silk opening screen and seamless site loading experience.

## ✨ Fullscreen Loading & Opening Screen

- **Fullscreen Immersion**: Covers 100% of the viewport edge-to-edge on both desktop and mobile devices.
- **Pleasant Silk Background Template**:
  - Warm champagne pearl backdrop with soft ambient lighting
  - Flowing satin drapery ripples and subtle moving sheen
  - Minimalist, distraction-free aesthetic
  - Handcrafted black calligraphy ribbon monogram
  - **KRYAA STYLES** in luxury Didone serif
  - Tagline: **WEAR CONFIDENTLY ✦**
- **Timing**:
  - Displays gracefully for **~3.8 seconds** (within the 3 to 5-second window), then automatically dissolves with a silky fade transition into the store.
  - Visitors can also tap or click anywhere to enter immediately without waiting.
